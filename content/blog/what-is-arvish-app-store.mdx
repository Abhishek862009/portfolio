If you have ever wished for small, simple apps that do one job and nothing else, the **Arvish App Store** is for you. It is a small collection of apps built by me, Abhishek (also known as Arvish), that you can download or open directly from [arvish.xyz/apps](https://www.arvish.xyz/apps).

It is not Google Play or the Apple App Store. It is a personal app store where every app is built by one developer, with no ads, no clutter and no extra features you did not ask for.

## What is the Arvish App Store?

The Arvish App Store is one place where all the apps I build live. Instead of searching for each app on different websites, you can see them all on a single page, pick the one you need, and start using it.

Every app has its own details page that explains what it does and how to use it.

## What is inside the App Store?

Right now the store has three apps, across three different types.

### 1. Abhi Gallery (Android)

Abhi Gallery is a normal, simple gallery app for Android. It has no extra features. You download the APK (about 64 MB) and install it on your phone.

If you just want a clean gallery to view your photos, this is the one.

### 2. Hisaab (Web app)

Hisaab is a simple hisaab diary, made for workers. You type the pieces (PS) and the rate (RS), then press Enter. Each line is multiplied and added to a running grand total.

- Lines can be edited or deleted.
- Entries are saved only in your browser.
- It opens directly in the browser, so there is nothing to install.

### 3. YT Saver (Terminal app)

YT Saver is a small app that runs on your own computer or phone. You paste a YouTube link, pick a quality from highest to lowest, and save the file.

It runs in a terminal on:

- Windows
- Android (Termux)
- Linux
- Mac

The YT Saver page walks you through the setup step by step. Please use it only for personal, offline use and respect the rights of creators.

## How does the App Store work?

- **Filter by type:** Browse All, Android, Web or Terminal apps.
- **Download or open:** Android apps come as an APK file, web apps open straight in your browser, and terminal apps come with a setup guide.
- **App details page:** Every app has its own page with a full description.

I am a developer who likes building things, and I wanted one place to put my work. Each app solves one small, real problem. The goal is to keep apps simple, useful and easy to use.
