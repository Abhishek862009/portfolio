You finally got a website. Great. Now comes the harder part: making sure a buyer in Germany, Dubai or the US can actually find it when they search.

Most small Indian exporters face the same problem. They build a site, share the link on WhatsApp, and then wonder why no one is finding them on Google. The truth is simple — Google doesn't know you exist yet.

## Why your brand name doesn't show up

When someone searches a new or uncommon name (like a brand you just created), Google often tries to "correct" it. It shows results for similar-sounding words instead. That's why many new businesses see "Did you mean..." suggestions instead of their own site.

This is normal. It happens to almost every new brand with low search volume and few links pointing to it.

## What actually works for small exporters

You don't need a big SEO agency. You need consistency. Here's the practical checklist:

### 1. Claim your name everywhere
- Use the exact same business name on your website, Google Business Profile, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, and LinkedIn.
- Add a short "About" that clearly says what you export and from where.

### 2. Get Google to notice your site
- Create a free Google Search Console account and submit your sitemap.
- Make sure every page has a clear title (example: "ABC Spices — Jaggery & Turmeric Exporters from India").

### 3. Put real content on the site
A one-page site is fine to start, but add:
- Product pages with photos and short descriptions
- Certifications (FSSAI, ISO, APEDA, etc.)
- A simple enquiry form or WhatsApp button

Buyers trust pages that look complete.

### 4. Get a few real mentions
Even 5–10 links from relevant places help:
- Export directories
- Local industry associations
- Supplier listings
- Your own LinkedIn and company page

### 5. Run a small brand search campaign (optional but effective)
Spend a little on Google Ads just for your exact brand name. This makes sure that when someone searches your name, your site appears at the top while organic rankings catch up.

## How long does this take?

For most small exporters, 4–8 weeks of consistent effort is enough for the brand name to start showing correctly. The more people search for you and click your site, the faster Google learns.
